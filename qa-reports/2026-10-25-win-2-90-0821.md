- An earlier try of that config with a `"..."` string and backslashes was invalid TOML (my mistake); tsumugi showed the toast `config.toml: line 3: missing escaped value ...` and kept working. With the plain `system = ["txt"]` config alone, `photo.png` also opened as `Code.exe --goto ...photo.png:1:1` (separate run, before the log script).
